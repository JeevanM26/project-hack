## 1. Tracks & Problem Statements

Each team chooses **exactly one primary track** and **one primary problem statement**. Teams may combine additional capabilities, but must clearly demonstrate the primary chosen approach.

### Track 1: Best Use of Gemma 4 (Our Selected Track)
*Meaningful use of Google Gemma 4 must be demonstrated.*

- **G1. Autonomous Multi-Agent Collaboration**:
  > *"Build a solution that incorporates multiple AI agents working together autonomously toward a shared objective. Demonstrate communication, information exchange, delegated responsibilities and coordinated actions."*
  - **Our Solution:** Single-Model Gemma 4 Dynamic Swarm with Co-Leader & Supervisor, System Architect, Lead Builder, on-demand specialist child sub-agents, and Shared Global Project Blackboard.

- **G2. Voice-First AI Interaction**:
  > *"Integrate voice-based intelligence that lets users interact naturally through speech. Demonstrate understanding of spoken requests, conversational context and meaningful responses or actions."*
  - **Our Solution:** Integrated Sarvam AI speech stack (Saaras v4 STT in `codemix` mode with technical keyterm prompting + Bulbul v4 Flash 24 kHz TTS) supporting English, Hinglish, and Bengaluru Kannada.

- **G3. Multimodal AI Intelligence**:
  > *"Demonstrate multimodal capabilities (vision, audio, code, documents) using Gemma 4."*

- **G5. Open Innovation with Gemma 4**:
  > *"Build an original solution in any domain using Gemma 4 as a core intelligence component."*
